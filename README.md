# Free CMA USA Mock Exam

Live at <https://ajmlbits-in.github.io/free-cma-usa-mock-exam/>
(repo: `free-cma-usa-mock-exam`).

Free CMA USA mock exams — Part 1 and Part 2 — with practice questions, flashcards,
revision notes and case-based questions. No login, no payment, no locks.

## The exams

- **Part 1** — 1,791 practice questions, 530 flashcards (5 per study unit),
  21 case-based question cases, 100-question mock exam. Built over 336 hours.
  Open it at [`/part1/`](part1/).
- **Part 2** — 5,421 practice questions, 412 flashcards, 21 quick revision notes,
  12 case-based question cases, 20 hard questions.
  Open it at [`/part2/`](part2/).
